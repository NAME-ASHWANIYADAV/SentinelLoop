# Red-Team Review (Phase 6)

Method: nine independent reviewers (compliance, ML validity and leakage, metric correctness, prior art, reliability and reproducibility, demo risk, safety and misleading claims, business value, submission format), each with read-only access to the whole workspace and short commands; every critical or high finding was then attacked by two sceptics with different lenses (reproduce it literally; is it already disclosed and would a judge care), and a completeness critic looked for what the nine missed. 98 agents, 1,391 tool calls, 48 minutes; raw output in `analysis/evidence/red_team_raw.json`, digest in `analysis/evidence/red_team_digest.txt`. Findings were written against the Phase 5 state of the project (2026-10-18, before any fix).

Totals: 157 findings (critical 3, high 44, low 48, medium 62); 44 critical/high findings verified by two sceptics each. Dispositions after Phase 6: DISCLOSED 18, FIXED 127, NOT A FINDING 1, OPEN (team) 6, PARTIAL 5.

Reading the tables: 'verified' is the two sceptics' corrected severity (refuted = the sceptic argued the finding away); 'disposition' is what this project did about it. Every FIXED item points at a file; DISCLOSED items are stated in `docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` or the threat model; OPEN items need the team.

## ML validity and leakage

Reviewer summary: I read the cleaning/feature/target/evaluation/conformal/OOF/anomaly/drift modules, the pipeline, all seven experiment scripts and configs, cross-checked every number in EXPERIMENT_RESULTS.md and REPRODUCIBILITY.md against the stored summary.json/metrics.json files, verified fold/calibration/tune-split disjointness and config hashes with one-liners, recomputed regime purity, reproduced the FD003 drift alarm from the stored monitor, and ran the fast test suite (97 passed). No fitted parameter is learned from test data and the OOF folds are engine-disjoint, but the worst problems are that (a) preprocessing/model variants (EMA, regime normalisation, asymmetric CQR) and the monitoring rules were selected by looking at official test-set results while the threat model claims "test used once", (b) the project's own E3 output shows the age-matched drift monitor raising a severe false alarm on 50 untouched FD003 test engines, contradicting the "0 false alarms" claim, and (c) controls the documents cite (tests/test_leakage.py, tuning-id assertions, 5-seed protocol) do not exist in the code. Verdict: the pipeline is leakage-clean at the parameter level but the experimental protocol over-uses the test set and several documented safeguards are unimplemented; the headline RMSEs reproduce, the drop-list line in REPRODUCIBILITY.md does not match the artefacts.
