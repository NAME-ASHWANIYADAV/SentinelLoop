| 7 | The agent evaluated as a maintenance policy (honest) | Demo & explanation 20 · Agent behavior | In maintenance terms: a late order = an engine that reaches end of life before the order is acted on; discarded life = good cycles given up by ordering early. E1 replay on complete out-of-fold trajectories: at the generic 25-cycle threshold neither policy is late on 3 of 4 subsets and the lower-bound policy discards 11–15 more cycles per engine; the frontier at matched late-rate targets is better on both metrics at 3/16 points, dominated at 3/16, a trade-off elsewhere; tuned lower-bound thresholds are unstable across seeds (6/2/0 cycles → 1–54% late). Cost sweep (assumed failure cost 25–500 cycles of life): point thresholds move from 8–13 to 10–17 cycles. E2: width-based abstention does not concentrate errors (acted-upon engines have equal or higher error rates on 3/4). E5: RMSE orders point models within one to two cycles of discarded life and cannot see the policy at all (same RMSE, 11–15 cycles apart). Message: the interval is a conservative operating point the operator chooses with the cost of failure in hand; we measured the trade-off instead of claiming a free lunch. Related work: Mitici 2023, Lee & Mitici 2023 (decision-level metrics), IntelliTwin (replay harness). | C10, C11, C15 |
