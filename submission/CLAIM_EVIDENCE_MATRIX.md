# Claim–Evidence Matrix (SentinelLoop, TELIPORT Season 4, Case Study 1)

Every claim that appears on the Round 1 slide, in the Round 2 deck plan, in the README or in the demo script is listed here with its status, the artefact that proves it and the command that regenerates that artefact. All numbers are those of the 2026-10-18 evidence run (`experiments/run_all.py`, log `experiments/results/run_all_phase6.log`) as printed by `experiments/numbers_digest.py` into `docs/NUMBERS.md`. Status vocabulary: VERIFIED (executed in this workspace, artefact stored), PARTIALLY VERIFIED (executed but with a stated caveat), PROPOSED (designed, not measured), NOT SUPPORTED (measured, hypothesis failed; reported as such), OPEN (team action). All data are NASA C-MAPSS simulations; no real-fleet or cost-saving claim is made anywhere.
