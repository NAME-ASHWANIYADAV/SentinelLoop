# Product Decision Log

Decisions taken while turning SentinelLoop into a product. Competition-era decisions D1–D26 remain in `docs/DECISION_LOG.md` and are not repeated here. Each entry records the decision, the evidence or reasoning, the alternatives considered and how the decision can be reversed.

| ID | Date | Decision | Why | Alternatives | Reversal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PD-01 | 2026-10-18 | Put the project under git: the competition version is committed on `main` and tagged `competition-baseline` (43050c6); all product work happens on the branch `product/platform`. Data, artefacts, models and databases stay out of git (`.gitignore`) and are backed up in `backups/competition_baseline_2026-10-18.zip` (81.8 MB, SHA-256 recorded next to it) | The product brief requires version control, branches and backups before any rewrite; the competition evidence must stay reproducible | Copying the folder (rejected: no history); committing artefacts (rejected: 100+ MB of regenerable binaries and a dataset that must not be redistributed) | `git switch main` restores the competition version; the zip restores artefacts |
| PD-02 | 2026-10-18 | Product documents live under `product/`; product evidence (logs, reports, test outputs) under `product/evidence/`; competition documents under `docs/` and `submission/` are frozen as history | Keeps competition framing (rounds, rubric) out of product claims | Rewriting `docs/` in place (rejected: loses the audit trail) | — |
| PD-03 | 2026-10-18 | Capability status vocabulary for every product claim: IMPLEMENTED_AND_TESTED, IMPLEMENTED_NOT_FIELD_VALIDATED, SIMULATED, PLANNED, BLOCKED, and NOT_SUPPORTED for claims our own evidence contradicts | The product brief requires every major capability to be classified; "tested" must never be read as "works on real equipment" | Free-text statuses (rejected: drift into marketing language) | — |
| PD-04 | 2026-10-18 | Phase 0 audit before any change to the application; the audit runs the existing workflows into a scratch directory so shipped evidence is untouched | Product brief: "Do not rewrite the application before completing this audit" | — | — |
| PD-05 | 2026-10-18 | Market and data research (Phase 1, Phase 2 dataset survey) runs as an independent sourced research workflow with a judge and an adversarial reviewer while the code audit proceeds; no statement from that research is used unless it carries a source URL that an agent opened | Research is independent of the code; the brief forbids invented market facts | Writing market documents from memory (rejected: unverifiable) | — |
| PD-06 | 2026-10-18 | Build the product as a **modular monolith** (`sentinelloop.app`): one Python package, SQLite in WAL mode with versioned, checksummed, forward-only migrations, a FastAPI REST layer. No microservices, message brokers or deep-learning services | Principles 4–5 (no complexity for appearance). One plant's maintenance data fits easily in SQLite; every service is unit-testable in-process. The migrations keep a later move to PostgreSQL mechanical | Microservices + Postgres + Kafka (rejected: operational weight with no requirement that needs it) | Port the SQL migrations to PostgreSQL; the service functions take a `Database` object |
| PD-07 | 2026-10-18 | Audit log = HMAC-SHA256 hash chain in the database plus an exportable anchor (latest seq and hash). The key must be at least 32 bytes; a development key needs explicit opt-in and every event signed with it is counted | A security review found a silent fallback to a development key. Tamper evidence must fail closed | Plain append-only table (rejected: undetectable edits); external ledger/blockchain (rejected, principle 4) | Rotate the key: re-anchor and record the key id per event |
| PD-08 | 2026-10-18 | Health states with **latching**: URGENT_REVIEW and INSPECTION_RECOMMENDED stay until completed maintenance or a reasoned dismissal by an allowed role. Bad data or model problems never hide an urgent state; DATA_QUALITY_FAILURE clears only after consecutive good assessments | A benign reading after an alarm is not evidence of repair. Alarm flicker destroys operator trust (R2 adoption research) | Re-derive the state from each assessment (rejected: an urgent asset could silently return to normal) | Thresholds in `configs/platform.yaml` |
| PD-09 | 2026-10-18 | **Scheduling defaults** from `SCHEDULING_EVALUATION.md` (SIMULATED): (a) for RUL models the default trigger is a threshold on predicted RUL, tuned per site on held-out history; the probabilistic trigger stays optional; (b) the default queue is `risk_greedy`; (c) the MILP is a what-if planner, recommended only once downtime is priced; (d) criticality-first ordering is not recommended under scarcity; (e) a site's downtime cost per hour is a required configuration input | Tuned threshold ≥ risk trigger in 5/6 scenarios; the risk trigger alone never wins (S1e); the risk-ranked queue wins under scarcity only while downtime is unpriced; the MILP wins once downtime > ~0.003–0.005 units/h; criticality-first is +63 to +83 under parts scarcity | Keep the risk trigger as default (rejected: not supported once baselines are tuned) | Change `DecisionConfig` / the policy file; re-run `product/experiments/s1*.py` on a site's history |
| PD-10 | 2026-10-18 | **Initial vertical: PROVISIONAL and GATED** (`INITIAL_MARKET_AND_ASSET_CHOICE.md`). Indian onshore wind: the party that runs maintenance on mid-life geared ~2.0–2.3 MW turbines and controls their 10-minute SCADA history; drivetrain and transformer faults first. Kept for a ~4-week gate period: G0 = pre-registered falsification test on CARE to Compare (engineering, `product/evidence/g0/PREREGISTRATION.md`); G1 = 3–5 access conversations (team; none held). If G1 fails or G0 fails, switch to the runner-up, State Transport Undertaking bus depots | The judge chose wind; the adversarial review rejected it as specified and required nine changes; after them buses lead the score table 25–24, but wind remains the only option with real field public data in the same asset class and data form, and both gates are cheap. Whether to keep the gate or switch now is the team lead's call (record §12) | Bus depots (data in a different form: SCANIA histograms vs job cards); campus chillers (no labelled public data) | Re-run the choice with the scores in V_judge_decision.md if customer access changes |
| PD-11 | 2026-10-18 | Wind models are **anomaly models** (normal-behaviour regression of drivetrain temperatures): no RUL and no failure probability. A sustained deviation → INSPECTION_RECOMMENDED; a severe sustained deviation → URGENT_REVIEW; the decision engine uses policy default due dates. The wind asset type has no generic `repair` job, so an urgent anomaly becomes a P1 inspection | Public wind data carry about 45 fault events and no run-to-failure trajectories, so RUL cannot be validated. Inventing a probability from an anomaly score would violate principle 1 | Calibrating anomaly scores to failure probabilities (rejected until enough labelled field failures exist) | Add a failure-time model family when labelled data allow; `classify(anomaly_only=...)` keeps both paths |
| PD-12 | 2026-10-18 | Each turbine must be **commissioned**: a reliability engineer declares a healthy period, the turbine's mean standardised residual per target is stored on the asset (audited, reason required) and subtracted before thresholds. Without a baseline the turbine is MODEL_OUT_OF_SCOPE | Without it, the fleet model false-alarmed on healthy synthetic turbines because of turbine-to-turbine thermal offsets (test `test_baseline_offsets_absorb_turbine_differences_but_not_a_developing_fault`). A per-turbine model per scope key would multiply registry entries | Shrinking the synthetic offsets (rejected: tunes the test data to pass); rolling self-baselines (rejected: absorbs slow faults) | Delete the baseline from the asset config; re-commission |
| PD-13 | 2026-10-18 | Model-specific data checks that do not depend on the operating regime (stuck sensors, gear relation on operating points) run **even when the model is out of scope**, so a stuck sensor on an idle turbine is reported as DATA_QUALITY_FAILURE. The C-MAPSS family keeps its regime-dependent conflict check gated behind scope | A stuck oil-temperature sensor during a calm day was reported only as "insufficient operation" (test `test_stuck_temperature_sensor_is_a_data_quality_failure_not_an_anomaly[12]`) | Run every model check regardless of scope (rejected: C-MAPSS regime normalisation is undefined out of regime) | `quality_independent_of_scope` flag per bundle class |
