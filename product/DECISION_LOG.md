| PD-28 | 2026-10-18 | **Design rules from the re-review of the Phase 9 fixes** (refines PD-16, PD-17, PD-18 and PD-20; `ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW.md` "Re-review of the fixes").<br>• **Orders and latches.** A data or review job never stands in for an inspection or urgent job: the needed order is raised as well. An older open order is linked to the current latch only if its job is the recommended, inspection or urgent job and it is awaiting approval or deferred; it then goes back for approval. Otherwise it is superseded by a new order that names it. Non-clearing jobs follow the site's configured data and review jobs on every completion path.<br>• **Rejections.** Rejection suppression applies on every creation path. It is keyed on the latched condition and the jobs that can stand in for it, and it does not carry over to a new latch episode.<br>• **Replays.** A replay may only **raise** a latch, never lower it or move the live state. The raised latch starts at wall-clock time, and the escalation is audited.<br>• **Wind model.** A stuck run anywhere in the scored window is a data failure. Sustain counts operating points.<br>• **Migrations.** Migration 0008 backfills replay protection on upgraded databases. Migration 0009 adds CMMS re-verification.<br>• **CMMS dispatch.** One dispatch per target, under an expiring lease. A create attempt is logged before the call, and only an ambiguous attempt counts as recovery evidence.<br>• **API.** `assess-range` is charged by its size and limited in concurrency. Clients cannot use the platform's idempotency-key prefixes. Decision configs are type- and range-checked, and `serve` refuses a config whose inspection job a registered asset type lacks (an urgent or data job the type lacks falls back to the inspection job by design). A malformed audit anchor is an error, never "no anchor" | The re-review (54 findings: 2 high and 14 medium after verification) showed that the first round of fixes was itself incomplete in places, and in one case regressed: linking any pre-latch order let a routine job clear an urgent warning.
