# Product Decision Log

Decisions taken while turning SentinelLoop into a product. Competition-era decisions D1–D26 remain in `docs/DECISION_LOG.md` and are not repeated here. Each entry records the decision, the evidence or reasoning, the alternatives considered and how the decision can be reversed.

| ID | Date | Decision | Why | Alternatives | Reversal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PD-01 | 2026-10-18 | Put the project under git: the competition version is committed on `main` and tagged `competition-baseline` (43050c6); all product work happens on the branch `product/platform`. Data, artefacts, models and databases stay out of git (`.gitignore`) and are backed up in `backups/competition_baseline_2026-10-18.zip` (81.8 MB, SHA-256 recorded next to it) | The product brief requires version control, branches and backups before any rewrite; the competition evidence must stay reproducible | Copying the folder (rejected: no history); committing artefacts (rejected: 100+ MB of regenerable binaries and a dataset that must not be redistributed) | `git switch main` restores the competition version; the zip restores artefacts |
| PD-02 | 2026-10-18 | Product documents live under `product/`; product evidence (logs, reports, test outputs) under `product/evidence/`; competition documents under `docs/` and `submission/` are frozen as history | Keeps competition framing (rounds, rubric) out of product claims | Rewriting `docs/` in place (rejected: loses the audit trail) | — |
| PD-03 | 2026-10-18 | Capability status vocabulary for every product claim: IMPLEMENTED_AND_TESTED, IMPLEMENTED_NOT_FIELD_VALIDATED, SIMULATED, PLANNED, BLOCKED, and NOT_SUPPORTED for claims our own evidence contradicts | The product brief requires every major capability to be classified; "tested" must never be read as "works on real equipment" | Free-text statuses (rejected: drift into marketing language) | — |
| PD-04 | 2026-10-18 | Phase 0 audit before any change to the application; the audit runs the existing workflows into a scratch directory so shipped evidence is untouched | Product brief: "Do not rewrite the application before completing this audit" | — | — |
| PD-05 | 2026-10-18 | Market and data research (Phase 1, Phase 2 dataset survey) runs as an independent sourced research workflow with a judge and an adversarial reviewer while the code audit proceeds; no statement from that research is used unless it carries a source URL that an agent opened | Research is independent of the code; the brief forbids invented market facts | Writing market documents from memory (rejected: unverifiable) | — |
