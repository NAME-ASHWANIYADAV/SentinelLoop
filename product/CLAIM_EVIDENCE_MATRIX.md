# Product Claim–Evidence Matrix

Every capability the product may claim, with its status, the evidence behind it and how to reproduce it. Status vocabulary (PD-03): **IMPLEMENTED_AND_TESTED** (code exists and automated tests or recorded runs exercise it; says nothing about real equipment), **IMPLEMENTED_NOT_FIELD_VALIDATED** (works on our data; never run on a customer's data or in a plant), **SIMULATED** (only demonstrated on simulated or synthetic data), **PLANNED**, **BLOCKED** (needs something we do not have), **NOT_SUPPORTED** (our own evidence contradicts it).

Data provenance labels used below: C-MAPSS = NASA simulated turbofan engines (public, simulated); INJECTED = faults we injected into C-MAPSS; SYNTHETIC = generated by our own code.

This version reflects the Phase 0 audit of the competition baseline (tag `competition-baseline`). It is updated at the end of every phase.
